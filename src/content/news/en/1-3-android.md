  - { platform: android, version: '1.3' }

- **More stations nearby:** Stations whose elevators are reported without a location now show up nearby too – U Klosterstraße, for example.
- **Widgets show only disruptions:** Widgets list only elevators that are out of service. When everything works, they show the Hissi mark.
- **Tidier interface:** Disruption alerts behind the bell, more room for your favorites list, a bigger nearby stations button and clearer notices.
- **More accessible:** Higher contrast, more room for large text and better TalkBack support.
